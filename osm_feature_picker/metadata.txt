about=Fetches OSM data for a bounding box (or the extent of a polygon layer) with tag filters and loads it as OGR/OSM sublayers (points/lines/multilinestrings/multipolygons/other_relations). Large areas are split into tiles and fetched one by one in a background thread with configurable pauses; each tile becomes its own layers immediately, and a tile-grid layer with a status field (pending/success/empty/failed) shows which tiles still need re-fetching. Tag-key autocomplete and per-area key/value discovery are included. Data (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL. Please follow the Overpass API usage policy.
about[ja]=緯度経度範囲(またはポリゴンレイヤの範囲)とタグフィルタを指定してOSMデータを取得し、OGR/OSMのサブレイヤとして読み込みます。広い範囲はタイルに分割し、バックグラウンドで間隔を空けて1タイルずつ取得します。取得できたタイルは即座にレイヤ化され、タイル境界レイヤのstatus列(pending/success/empty/failed)で再取得が必要な範囲を確認できます。タグキーの入力補完や、範囲内のキー/値候補の取得機能もあります。データ (c) OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL。Overpass APIの利用ポリシーに従ってください。
